範例名片（虛構資料）

手邊沒有名片時，可以用這兩張示範名片試試 BBCARD拍板。公司、人名、電話和 Email 都是虛構的。

怎麼用

  1. 在 iPhone 的 Safari 打開這一頁，長按下面的名片圖，選「加入照片」。
  2. 打開 BBCARD拍板，到「掃描」分頁，點「從相簿選」，選剛存的名片圖。
  3. 第一次會先問你是否同意 AI 辨識，按「同意並繼續」；等幾十秒，名片就會出現在「聯絡人」。

Sample cards (made-up details)

No business card at hand? Try BBCARD with these two sample cards. The companies, names, phone numbers and emails are all made up.

How to use

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone, touch and hold a card picture below, and choose “Add to Photos”.
  2. Open BBCARD, go to the “Scan” tab, tap “Pick from gallery” and choose the card you saved.
  3. The first time, the app asks whether the photo may be read by AI: tap “Agree and continue”. After up to a minute the card appears on the Contacts tab.
