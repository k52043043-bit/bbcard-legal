三步驟，從一疊名片到看懂客戶Three steps from a stack of cards to a clear picture

名片收進來，建檔與整理交給 App。Cards go in; the app does the filing.

  1. 1

    拍名片Snap the cards

    用相機連拍，或從相簿挑；一次可以排入上百張名片或組織圖，App 開著時在背景一張張辨識。Shoot them in bursts or pick photos from your library. Queue up hundreds of cards or org charts at once; they are read one after another in the background while the app is open.

  2. 2

    自動建檔Contacts, filed

    同一位聯絡人的名片再掃一次，會依公司與姓名認出來，只補上缺的欄位，不重複建檔；職稱或部門有變動時更新，並記進個人歷程。Scan a contact’s card again and the app recognizes them by company and name: no duplicate, only the missing fields are filled in. A changed title or department is updated and logged in their history.

  3. 3

    看懂誰拍板See who decides

    幫每個人標上拍板、影響、支持或反對，關係圖就依組織與影響力畫出每家客戶，一眼看懂；缺決策者會標紅提醒。Tag each person as decider, influencer, supporter or blocker, and the map draws every customer by organization and influence. A missing decision maker is flagged in red.

圍繞業務的一天設計Built around a salesperson’s day

從認識客戶、釐清誰拍板，到拜訪、電話與下一次跟進。From meeting a customer and working out who decides, to the visit, the call and the next follow-up.

拍名片，自動建檔Scan a card, get a contact

連拍、相簿、組織圖都行，App 開著時在背景一張張辨識；每月有辨識張數上限，超過的先留著，有張數後再讀。沒有名片，也能貼上 Email 簽名檔，或掃數位名片 QR（vCard／MECARD），確認後新增。Burst-shoot, pick from your library or photograph an org chart; cards are read one after another while the app is open. Monthly reading has a cap: extra photos wait until there is room. No card? Paste an email signature or scan a digital-card QR code (vCard or MECARD) and confirm.

看懂決策鏈See who decides

幫每個人標上拍板、影響、支持或反對，誰決策、誰影響一目了然；缺決策者會標紅提醒。在關係圖上點人就能寫附註，也能存成圖檔（SVG）放進簡報。Tag each person as decider, influencer, supporter or blocker and see it at a glance; a missing decision maker is flagged in red. Tap a person to add a note, and save the map as an image (SVG) for your slides.

今天該聯絡誰Who to contact today

一張清單排好：該跟進的、太久沒聯絡的決策者、剛換公司的老窗口；聯絡過就自動消失。One sorted list: follow-ups due, decision makers you haven’t spoken to for a while, old contacts who just changed company. It clears itself once you’ve been in touch.

拜訪打卡Check in at visits

勾選當天見到的人、記一句談了什麼，客戶關係圖下方的拜訪卡自動列出拜訪紀錄（不記錄位置）。Tick who you met and note what you discussed; the visit card under the customer’s map lists your visits (your location is not recorded).

電話與跟進Calls and follow-ups

從 App 撥完電話，一鍵記下結果並設下次跟進，時間到手機提醒你；從 App 寄完信，一鍵記下已寄出。公司電話有分機時，撥號前會先提醒。After a call placed from the app, log the result in one tap and set the next follow-up; your phone reminds you on time. After an email, log it as sent in one tap. When an office number has an extension, you get a reminder before dialing.

一鍵查統編Taiwan tax ID lookup

用公司名稱查經濟部公開資料，整家公司一起補。Look up a company’s tax ID from its name using the Ministry of Economic Affairs’ public data and fill in the whole company at once.

個人與團隊Personal and team

個人空間只有你看得到；也可以加入團隊與同事共用名冊。多人同時記備註不會互相蓋掉，改同一位聯絡人的不同欄位也都保留（改同一欄時以最後送出的為準）。Your personal space is visible only to you. You can also join a team and share the contact list. Notes added at the same time never overwrite each other, and edits to different fields of one contact are all kept (if two people change the same field, the last one sent wins).

資料你做主Your data, your call

照片交給 AI 辨識前先問你，隨時可撤回；名冊可隨時匯出成 CSV 或聯絡人名片檔，也能在 App 裡刪除帳號與資料；可用臉部辨識或指紋鎖住 App。We ask before a photo goes to AI, and you can withdraw any time. Export your contact list (CSV or vCard) whenever you like, delete your account and data inside the app, and lock the app with face or fingerprint unlock.

資料放哪裡、誰看得到Where your data lives, and who sees it

名片上是別人的資料，所以我們把話說清楚。Cards carry other people’s details, so we keep this plain.

  • 名冊與名片照片存放在 BBCARD 的雲端資料庫，只有你（和你加入的團隊）在 App 裡看得到。Your contact list and card photos are stored in BBCARD’s cloud database and can only be seen in the app by you (and the team you join).
  • 第一次辨識名片前，App 會先問你是否同意把照片交給 AI 服務讀出文字。不同意也能用：到「掃描」›「其他方式加人」自己輸入聯絡人；同意後隨時可在「設定 › AI 辨識」撤回。Before the first card is read, the app asks whether it may send the photo to an AI service to read the text. If you say no, you can still add contacts yourself under Scan › Other ways to add someone, and you can withdraw your agreement any time under Settings › AI recognition.
  • 沒有廣告，沒有第三方分析或當機回報工具，不收集廣告 ID，不讀取手機通訊錄和位置。No ads, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, no advertising ID, and we don’t read your phone’s contacts or location.
  • 隨時可以匯出名冊（CSV 或聯絡人名片檔），也可以在 App 裡刪除帳號與資料。Export your contact list (CSV or vCard) any time, and delete your account and data inside the app.

常見問題Questions

找不到答案？到支援頁看看，或寫信給我們。Can’t find your answer? See Support or write to us.

BBCARD拍板是什麼？What is BBCARD?

給業務用的名片與客戶管理 App：拍名片自動建檔，標好每個人的角色後畫出每家客戶的決策關係圖，並記錄拜訪、電話與跟進。A business-card and customer-management app for salespeople: scan cards into contacts, tag each person’s role to get each customer’s decision map, and log visits, calls and follow-ups.

一定要同意 AI 辨識嗎？Do I have to agree to AI reading?

不用。不同意也能使用：到「掃描」›「其他方式加人」自己輸入聯絡人；同意後可以隨時在「設定 › AI 辨識」撤回。No. You can still use the app and add contacts yourself under Scan › Other ways to add someone. If you agree, you can withdraw any time under Settings › AI recognition.

我的名片資料誰看得到？Who can see my contacts?

個人空間只有你看得到。加入團隊後，團隊名冊由同一個團隊的成員共用，由負責人決定誰加入。細節見隱私權政策。Your personal space is visible only to you. In a team, the roster is shared by the team’s members, and the owner decides who joins. Details are in the privacy policy.

怎麼刪除帳號與資料？How do I delete my account and data?

在 App 裡到「設定 › 隱私與條款 › 刪除資料與帳號」即可；登不進去也可以來信。步驟見刪除帳號說明。In the app go to Settings › Privacy & terms › Delete data & account. If you can’t sign in, write to us. The steps are in how to delete your account.

收不到驗證碼怎麼辦？The sign-up code didn’t arrive.

先看垃圾郵件匣，等一分鐘再按「重寄驗證碼」；還是收不到，請來信，我們會協助。Check your spam folder, wait a minute and tap “Send the code again”; if it still doesn’t come, write to us and we’ll help.

目前為測試階段，名額有限。請寫信給我們，附上你的 Email 與手機型號。BBCARD is in testing with limited places. Write to us with your email and phone model.
