服務條款

生效日期：2026/10/16

服務內容

BBCARD拍板提供名片與組織圖辨識、聯絡人名冊、關係圖、個人歷程與待辦提醒，個人和團隊都可以用。

你的名冊

  • 名冊裡的資料由你（團隊名冊由團隊）負責。請遵守個人資料保護法，只蒐集和業務往來有關的聯絡資料。
  • 名片上他人的資料，由你（或你的團隊）決定蒐集與利用：你須有合法的依據，並依法告知當事人；我們只依你的指示代為處理。不得輸入病歷、健康、犯罪紀錄等特種個資。

方案

免費版：每月 20 張，功能全部開放。

張數怎麼算

每辨識完成一張照片（名片或組織圖）算 1 張；辨識失敗不計。

使用限制

不得用於騷擾、發送垃圾訊息或違法蒐集個人資料；不得嘗試存取別人的資料。

辨識結果

AI 辨識可能有錯，重要資訊請自行確認。除故意或重大過失外，我們不對直接使用辨識結果造成的損失負責。

年齡

你須年滿 18 歲才能使用本 App。

帳號終止

你違反本條款時，我們可以在通知後暫停或終止你的帳號；你也可以隨時自己刪除帳號。

準據法

本條款適用中華民國法律；因本服務產生的爭議，以法律規定的管轄法院為準。

Terms of service

Effective: 2026/10/16

The service

BBCARD reads business cards and org charts and keeps a contact roster with a relationship map, history and reminders, for one person or a team.

Your roster

  • You (and, for a team roster, the team) are responsible for its data. Follow privacy law and collect only business contact details.
  • You (or your team) decide what is collected and used about the people on the cards: you must have a lawful basis and inform them as the law requires, and we process the data only on your instructions. Don’t enter sensitive data such as medical, health or criminal records.

Plans

Free: 20 cards a month, with every feature open.

What counts as a card

Each photo (card or org chart) recognised counts as one card; failed recognitions are not counted.

Acceptable use

No harassment, spam, unlawful collection of personal data, or attempts to reach other people’s data.

Recognition

AI recognition can make mistakes; check important details. Except for intent or gross negligence, we are not liable for losses from relying on a result directly.

Age

You must be 18 or older to use the app.

Ending an account

If you break these terms we may suspend or close your account after telling you; you can delete your account yourself at any time.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of China (Taiwan); disputes go to the court the law gives jurisdiction.
