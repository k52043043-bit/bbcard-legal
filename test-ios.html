測試者 iOS 教學

用 TestFlight 安裝 BBCARD拍板測試版，約 10 分鐘。目前 iPhone 測試版：1.0.0（Build 5）。

流程
裝 TestFlight、接受邀請、安裝 BBCARD、註冊登入、測 6 件事、回報問題
邀請連結
在賴泯佑傳給你的訊息裡（要用 iPhone 點）
有問題找
賴泯佑。App 裡「設定」點「意見回饋」最快；也可以寄信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw

壹、先確認三件事

1-1 你的 iPhone

  • 機型：iPhone 8 或更新。系統：iOS 16.4 以上。
  • 怎麼看系統版本：設定、一般、軟體更新。版本太舊就先更新。

1-2 你需要準備

  • 一個 Apple 帳號（平常在 App Store 下載 App 用的那個）。
  • 一個收得到信的 Email（註冊 BBCARD 要收驗證碼）。
  • 網路（Wi-Fi 或行動網路都可以）。

1-3 先知道

這是「測試版」，還在開發，可能有錯誤或畫面不完整，這正是需要你幫忙找出來的地方。測試期間你輸入的資料會存在 BBCARD 的雲端。名片辨識時，照片會經 BBCARD 的伺服器交給 Google 的 AI（Gemini，必要時改由 Anthropic 的 Claude）讀出文字，所以請用你同意拍的名片，或用範例名片，不要拿客戶機密資料來試。細節見隱私權政策。

這一版 iPhone 測試版在第一次拍名片時，不會另外跳出同意視窗：照片送去辨識，就代表你已經看過上面這段說明。

貳、安裝：看圖照著按（3 步驟，約 5 分鐘）

下面的圖是示意圖，不是實際畫面，你的手機看起來可能略有不同。紅色圈圈是要點的地方；圖示以名稱和開發者為準（TestFlight，開發者是 Apple）。

文字說明

  1. 步驟 1：打開 App Store，搜尋「TestFlight」（開發者是 Apple），點「取得」。已經裝過就跳過。
  2. 步驟 2：一定要在 iPhone 上點邀請連結（LINE、Email 都可以），用電腦開沒有用。Email 邀請的話，點信裡的「在 TestFlight 中檢視」。TestFlight 開啟後，點「接受」。
  3. 步驟 3：點「安裝」，等進度跑完，再點「開啟」。之後桌面上會出現 BBCARD拍板 的圖示，和一般 App 一樣使用。

參、第一次使用 BBCARD

3-1 註冊帳號與允許權限

  1. 開啟後點「註冊新帳號」，輸入 Email，設定密碼（至少 8 個字，要有英文字母和數字）。
  2. 按「註冊，寄驗證碼」，到信箱找 6 位數驗證碼輸入，再按「完成註冊」。沒收到的話先看垃圾郵件。
  3. 進去之後選「個人使用」就能開始。如果賴泯佑給了 8 碼邀請碼，要加入團隊，就選「團隊使用」並輸入邀請碼。
  • 相機：第一次拍名片時手機會問，請按「好」。通知：每天提醒要用到，建議允許。

肆、請幫忙測這 6 件事

  1. 拍名片

    怎麼做點下方「掃描」，再點「拍名片」。把名片放桌上、手機對準，拿穩就會自動拍。一疊名片可以一張接一張拍，拍完按「儲存」。

    正常的樣子名片在背景上傳、辨識，1～2 分鐘後出現在「聯絡人」，姓名、公司、電話都對。

  2. 看名片卡

    怎麼做在「聯絡人」點一位。

    正常的樣子資料完整，打電話、寄信、地圖的按鈕點得動。

  3. 搜尋

    怎麼做在「聯絡人」上方搜尋框打字。也可以點麥克風，用 iPhone 鍵盤上的麥克風說話（要先到 設定、一般、鍵盤，開啟「啟用聽寫」）。

    正常的樣子找得到人。說「某某公司的決策者」會自動設好條件。

  4. 待辦與提醒

    怎麼做名片缺資料時，點「聯絡人」右下角的待辦，一位一位補。再到 BBCARD 的「設定」打開「每天提醒」。

    正常的樣子隔天早上收到通知，點通知會打開「今天要聯絡」的清單。

  5. 關係圖

    怎麼做點下方「關係圖」，選一個客戶。

    正常的樣子看得到每家公司誰決策、誰影響，點人可以看資料。

  6. 語言與外觀

    怎麼做到「設定」，換語言、換淺色或深色。

    正常的樣子整個畫面馬上跟著換，沒有跑版或看不到的字。

不會用的功能，到「設定」點「使用教學」有圖文說明。免費版每月辨識 20 張，要多測可到「設定」的「方案」，用「測試版：免費試用」，不用付費。沒有名片可以用範例名片。

伍、TestFlight 日常使用

5-1 看剩幾天、有新版就更新

打開 TestFlight 的首頁，就能看到 BBCARD拍板 的測試還剩幾天。每個測試版只能用 90 天，天數歸零就打不開，請賴泯佑上傳新版。有新版本時，TestFlight 會通知你，按鈕會變成「更新」，按下去就好。建議允許 TestFlight 的通知。

5-2 遇到問題怎麼回報

  • 方法 A（最簡單）：直接在 BBCARD 裡回報。到「設定」點「意見回饋」，寫一兩句，可以附截圖（最多 3 張），按「送出」，會自動附上 App 版本和手機型號。
  • 方法 B：照上面的圖 2、圖 3 用 TestFlight 回報。也可以打開 TestFlight，點 BBCARD拍板，再點「傳送測試版意見回饋」。
  • 方法 C：App 閃退之後，TestFlight 會問你要不要回報，請按「是」。

回報時請寫三件事：你做了什麼（按了哪裡）、你以為會怎麼樣、實際發生了什麼（有截圖更好）。

5-3 不想再測了

  • 打開 TestFlight，點 BBCARD拍板，往下找「停止測試」。
  • 想刪掉 BBCARD 的帳號，到「設定」的「隱私與條款」，點紅字「刪除資料與帳號」。

陸、常見問題

點連結沒反應，或說無法加入

確認是用 iPhone 點的、系統是 iOS 16.4 以上。還是不行，可能是邀請滿了或失效，請找賴泯佑。

沒收到邀請信

先看垃圾郵件。也可以直接用賴泯佑傳給你的連結。

顯示「此 Beta 版已過期」

每個測試版只能用 90 天，期限到就打不開。請賴泯佑上傳新版，再從 TestFlight 更新。

登入失敗、忘記密碼

在登入頁點「忘記密碼／還沒設過密碼」，用 Email 收驗證碼重設。

辨識張數用完了

免費版每月 20 張。到「設定」的「方案」，用「測試版：免費試用」可以免費試更高的方案。

謝謝你幫忙測試！你回報的每一個問題，都會讓正式版更穩。

iPhone tester guide

Install the BBCARD test version with TestFlight in about 10 minutes. Current iPhone test version: 1.0.0 (Build 5).

Steps
Install TestFlight, accept the invitation, install BBCARD, sign up, try 6 things, report problems
Invitation link
In the message Vito sent you (open it on your iPhone)
Questions
Vito. The fastest way is Settings › Feedback inside the app; you can also email k52043043@yahoo.com.tw

1. Before you start

1-1 Your iPhone

  • Model: iPhone 8 or newer. System: iOS 16.4 or later.
  • To check: Settings, General, Software Update. Update first if yours is older.

1-2 What you need

  • An Apple Account (the one you use to download apps).
  • An email address you can read (BBCARD sends a sign-up code).
  • An internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

1-3 Good to know

This is a test version still in development: it may have errors or unfinished screens, and finding them is exactly where we need your help. What you enter while testing is stored in BBCARD’s cloud. To read a card, its photo goes through BBCARD’s server to Google’s AI (Gemini, or Anthropic’s Claude if needed) that reads the text, so please use cards you are allowed to photograph, or the sample cards, and nothing confidential from a customer. Details are in the privacy policy.

In this iPhone test version no separate consent window appears when you scan your first card: sending a photo for reading means you have read the paragraph above.

2. Install: follow the pictures (3 steps, about 5 minutes)

The pictures are illustrations, not real screens, so your phone may look slightly different (the words inside them are in Chinese). The red circles show where to tap; go by the name and developer (TestFlight, by Apple).

In words

  1. Step 1: open the App Store, search “TestFlight” (developer: Apple) and tap “Get”. Skip this if you already have it.
  2. Step 2: open the invitation link on your iPhone (from LINE or email); it does nothing on a computer. If it came by email, tap “View in TestFlight” in the message. When TestFlight opens, tap “Accept”.
  3. Step 3: tap “Install”, wait for the progress to finish, then tap “Open”. A BBCARD icon appears on your home screen and works like any other app.

3. Using BBCARD for the first time

3-1 Sign up and allow permissions

  1. Open the app and tap “Create an account”. Enter your email and choose a password (at least 8 characters, with letters and digits).
  2. Tap “Sign up and send the code”, find the 6-digit code in your inbox, enter it and tap “Finish signing up”. If it doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder.
  3. Choose “Personal use” and you are ready. If Vito gave you an 8-character invitation code to join a team, choose “Team use” and enter it.
  • Camera: the first time you scan a card the phone asks; tap OK. Notifications: the daily reminder needs them, so please allow.

4. Please try these 6 things

  1. Scan a card

    HowTap “Scan” at the bottom, then “Scan cards”. Lay the card on a desk and hold the phone steady over it: it shoots by itself. A whole stack can go one after another; tap “Save” when you are done.

    You should seeThe cards upload and are read in the background; after a minute or two they appear under “Contacts” with the right name, company and phone number.

  2. Open a contact

    HowOn the Contacts tab, tap a person.

    You should seeThe details are complete, and the call, email and map buttons work.

  3. Search

    HowType in the search box at the top of Contacts. You can also tap the microphone and speak with the dictation key of the iPhone keyboard (turn on “Enable Dictation” first, under Settings, General, Keyboard).

    You should seeYou find the person. Saying “the decision maker at (a company)” sets the filter for you.

  4. To-dos and reminders

    HowWhen a card is missing details, tap the to-do button at the bottom right of Contacts and fill them in one by one. Then turn on the daily reminder in BBCARD’s Settings.

    You should seeThe next morning you get a notification; tapping it opens the list of people to contact today.

  5. Relationship map

    HowTap “Map” at the bottom and pick a customer.

    You should seeYou see who decides and who influences at each company; tap a person for their details.

  6. Language and look

    HowIn Settings, switch the language and the light or dark look.

    You should seeThe whole screen follows at once, with nothing cut off or unreadable.

For anything you are unsure of, open Settings › How to use for pictures and explanations. The free plan reads 20 cards a month; to test more, go to Settings › Plan and use “Test build: free trial”, no payment needed. No cards at hand? Use the sample cards.

5. Using TestFlight day to day

5-1 Days left, and updating

On the TestFlight home screen you can see how many days of testing BBCARD has left. Each test version works for 90 days; when the count reaches zero it no longer opens and Vito has to upload a new one. When there is a new version TestFlight notifies you and the button becomes “Update”; just tap it. We suggest allowing TestFlight notifications.

5-2 How to report a problem

  • Method A (easiest): report inside BBCARD. Go to Settings › Feedback, write a line or two, attach screenshots if you like (up to 3) and tap “Send”; the app version and phone model are added automatically.
  • Method B: report through TestFlight as in pictures 2 and 3. You can also open TestFlight, tap BBCARD, then tap “Send Beta Feedback”.
  • Method C: after the app crashes, TestFlight asks whether to report it; please tap Yes.

Please say three things: what you did (what you tapped), what you expected, and what actually happened (a screenshot helps).

5-3 When you want to stop

  • Open TestFlight, tap BBCARD and scroll down to “Stop Testing”.
  • To delete your BBCARD account, go to Settings › Privacy & terms and tap the red “Delete data & account”.

6. Common questions

The link does nothing, or says I can’t join

Make sure you opened it on an iPhone running iOS 16.4 or later. If it still fails, the invitation may be full or expired: ask Vito.

No invitation email

Check your spam folder. You can also use the link Vito sent you.

It says “This beta has expired”

Each test version lasts 90 days. Ask Vito to upload a new one, then update it from TestFlight.

I can’t sign in or forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password / no password yet” and use the emailed code to set a new one.

The reading allowance ran out

The free plan reads 20 cards a month. Under Settings › Plan, “Test build: free trial” lets you try a higher plan for free.

Thank you for testing! Every problem you report makes the real version more solid.
