隱私權政策

生效日期：2026/10/16

我們是誰

BBCARD拍板（以下稱本 App）由 VitoLai 賴泯佑 提供。有任何隱私問題請寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw。

我們處理哪些資料

  • 你拍攝或選取的名片、組織圖照片。拍照用的是手機內建的文件掃描功能（Android 是 Google Play 服務的文件掃描器，iPhone 是內建的文件掃描；用不了時改用 App 內建相機），找邊、拉正都在手機上處理。
  • 你替某位聯絡人另外加的照片。
  • 從照片辨識出的聯絡人資料（姓名、公司、部門、職稱、電話、Email、地址等），以及你輸入的備註、標籤、待辦、個人歷程和停車場。這些多半是名片上「別人」的資料。
  • 帳號資料：註冊的 Email、密碼（只保存加密過的雜湊值，任何人都看不到原文）、顯示名稱、負責業務代號。
  • 團隊資料：團隊成員的 Email、角色、負責業務代號和邀請紀錄。
  • 意見回饋：你寫的內容、附上的截圖，以及 App 版本、系統版本、手機型號和你是在哪個畫面寄出的。
  • 方案與用量：方案、每月辨識張數。
  • 伺服器紀錄：服務供應商會記下連線的 IP 位址和時間，約保存 7 天，用來維運和防止濫用；登入系統（Supabase Auth）另外記錄登入事件（可能含 Email 與 IP 位址），由供應商保存，要清除請寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw。
  • 我們不讀取手機通訊錄和位置，不收集廣告 ID，沒有廣告，也沒有第三方分析或當機回報工具。

資料存在哪裡

  • 名冊、照片、帳號和團隊資料都存在 BBCARD拍板 的雲端資料庫（由 Supabase 代管，機房在日本東京）；照片放在不公開的儲存區。
  • 你只會在 App 裡看到自己的個人空間和所屬團隊的資料，不會、也不需要進到資料庫。資料庫的每一筆資料都設了存取規則：個人空間只有你看得到，團隊名冊只有團隊成員看得到。開發者只在維運、處理你的請求，或法律要求時才會查看資料。
  • 手機上會保留一份暫存，讓你沒有網路也能查、能記，連上後自動送出；登出時清除。
  • 名片辨識時，照片會經本 App 的伺服器傳給 Google 的 Gemini AI（付費 API）讀出文字，不附你的帳號資料，只用來把文字傳回來。依 Google 付費 API 的條款，Google 不拿這些內容改善產品（訓練模型），但可能為了偵測濫用而短期記錄，並可能在其他國家暫存。Gemini 用不了或讀不出內容時，可能改由 Anthropic 的 Claude 處理（同樣只用來回傳文字）。
  • 第一次辨識前，本 App 會先把這些告訴你並徵求你的同意；不同意也能用，改成手動新增聯絡人。同意後可以隨時在「設定 › AI 辨識」撤回。
  • 用「查統編」時，只會把公司名稱送到經濟部商工登記公示資料（政府公開資料），不含任何聯絡人的個人資料。
  • 註冊和重設密碼的驗證碼，由本 App 的寄信信箱（Google Gmail）寄出；信件副本（含你的 Email 與驗證碼）會留在該信箱的寄件備份。

資料的用途

只用來提供名片辨識、聯絡人管理、關係圖、個人歷程、待辦提醒，以及處理你送出的意見回饋。不出售、不出租，也不用在廣告上。

和誰分享

  • 不出售、不出租。只有以下服務供應商會依我們的指示代為處理資料：Supabase（資料庫與照片儲存）、Google Gemini 和必要時的 Anthropic Claude（辨識照片）。它們各有自己的資料保護條款，所提供的保護不低於本政策所述；驗證碼信件則經 Google Gmail 寄出（見上）。同一個團隊的成員看得到團隊名冊，由負責人決定誰加入。
  • 你按「導航」「附近停車場」「新聞」「LinkedIn」時，所選聯絡人的地址、公司名稱或姓名會由你的手機交給 Google 地圖、Google 新聞或 LinkedIn（我們不經手）。
  • 你按撥號、寄信（一次寄給多位聯絡人時用密件副本）、分享或匯出時，內容會交給你選的 App（電話、郵件或分享對象）；語音搜尋在 iPhone 用鍵盤的聽寫（Apple 處理），在 Android 交給 Google 的語音輸入，本 App 都不接觸麥克風。

你的權利

你可以查詢、閱覽、索取複本、更正、要求停止處理或刪除你的個人資料：在 App 裡操作，或寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw，我們會在法定期限內回覆。不提供 Email 就無法註冊；不同意 AI 辨識，仍可手動輸入聯絡人。

名片上的人

如果你的資料被某位使用者存進本 App（例如你給了對方名片），請先聯絡那位使用者；也可以寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw，我們會協助。發生個資外洩時，我們會依法通知受影響的人，並通報主管機關。

匯出

你可以隨時在 App 裡把名冊匯出（CSV 或聯絡人名片檔），帶到其他地方使用。

保存與刪除

  • 帳號資料保存到你刪除帳號為止。在「設定 › 隱私與條款 › 刪除資料與帳號」可以清除這支手機上的資料，也可以刪除帳號；刪除帳號會一起刪除你個人空間的名冊和照片，你在團隊裡建的資料留在團隊。
  • 刪除一位聯絡人時，他會先從名冊移除並標示為已刪除（讓你的其他手機同步）；已刪除的聯絡人和名片照片仍留在伺服器，直到你刪除帳號（個人空間），或團隊被刪除（團隊空間）為止，想提前清除請寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw。辨識完成後伺服器暫存的辨識結果和操作紀錄（誰在何時改了什麼）也一樣。伺服器的每日備份最多再保留 7 天。
  • 組織圖和讀不出來、沒有存成聯絡人的照片，保存在你的空間資料夾，直到你刪除帳號（團隊空間裡的，到團隊被刪除）為止。
  • 團隊負責人要先把團隊移交給別人，才能刪除帳號。
  • 也可以寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw 申請刪除帳號與資料。
  • 團隊方案的合約與帳單紀錄（公司名稱、統一編號、發票 Email、金額）依法保存，不會隨帳號刪除。
  • 團隊方案到期後先保留 90 天（期間可以查看、匯出），之後無法再開啟；要刪除請寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw。
  • 負責人或管理員把成員標成離職或移出團隊後，對方從下一次連線起就看不到團隊名冊。

安全

傳輸全程加密（HTTPS）；每一筆資料都有存取規則；註冊時用 Email 驗證碼確認本人；可以開啟指紋或臉部辨識。

年齡

本 App 是商務用途，限 18 歲以上使用，不提供給兒童。

政策變更

有重大變更時會更新本頁和生效日期。本政策生效日期：2026/10/16。

Privacy policy

Effective: 2026/10/16

Who we are

BBCARD is provided by VitoLai 賴泯佑. Privacy questions: k52043043@yahoo.com.tw.

What we handle

  • Photos of business cards and org charts you take or pick (with the phone’s own document scanner — Google Play services on Android, the built-in one on iPhone — or the app’s camera where that can’t run; edges are found and straightened on the phone).
  • Other photos you add to a contact.
  • Contact details recognised from them (name, company, department, title, phone, email, address…) and the notes, tags, to-dos, history and parking you add. Most of this is other people’s details, taken from the cards.
  • Account: the email you sign up with, your password (only a salted hash is kept; nobody can read it), display name and rep code.
  • Team: members’ emails, roles, rep codes and invitations.
  • Feedback: what you write, the screenshots you attach, and the app version, system version, phone model and screen you sent it from.
  • Plan and usage: plan and cards read each month.
  • Server logs: our service providers log connection IP addresses and times for about 7 days, to run the service and prevent abuse; the sign-in system (Supabase Auth) also records sign-in events (possibly with your email and IP address), kept by the provider: write to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw to have them cleared.
  • We don’t read your phone’s contacts or location, collect advertising IDs, show ads, or use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Where it lives

  • Rosters, photos, accounts and teams are kept in BBCARD’s cloud database (run by Supabase, in Tokyo); photos are in private storage.
  • You see your personal space and your team’s data only in the app, never in the database itself. Every record has access rules: your personal space is yours alone, a team’s roster is its members’. The developer looks at data only to run the service, to handle your request, or when the law requires.
  • The phone keeps a copy so you can look things up and take notes offline; it sends them when you’re back online, and the copy is cleared when you sign out.
  • To read a card, its photo is sent through our server to Google’s Gemini AI (paid API), without your account details, and only to return the text. Under Google’s paid-API terms, Google does not use this content to improve its products (train models), but may log it briefly to detect abuse and may cache it in other countries. If Gemini is unavailable or can’t read it, Anthropic’s Claude may read it instead (again only to return the text).
  • Before your first scan the app tells you this and asks for your agreement; if you say no, you can still add contacts by hand. If you agree, you can withdraw any time under Settings › AI recognition.
  • Looking up a tax ID sends only the company name to the Taiwan company registry (government open data), never anyone’s personal details.
  • Codes for signing up and resetting a password are sent from the app’s own mailbox (Google Gmail); a copy of each message (with your email address and the code) stays in that mailbox’s sent folder.

How it is used

Only to provide card recognition, contacts, the relationship map, history and reminders, and to handle the feedback you send. Never sold, rented or used for ads.

Sharing

  • Never sold or rented. Only these service providers process data on our instructions: Supabase (database and photo storage), Google Gemini and, if needed, Anthropic Claude (reading photos). Each has its own data-protection terms, and the protection it gives is not lower than this policy describes; sign-up codes are mailed through Google Gmail (see above). Members of a team see its roster; the owner decides who joins.
  • When you tap navigation, parking, news or LinkedIn, the chosen contact’s address, company name or name is handed by your phone to Google Maps, Google News or LinkedIn (we never see it).
  • When you tap call, email (several contacts at once go in blind copy), share or export, the content is handed to the app you choose (phone, mail or whoever you share with); voice search uses the keyboard’s dictation on iPhone (handled by Apple) and Google’s voice input on Android, and the app never touches the microphone.

Your rights

You can ask to see, copy, correct, stop the processing of, or delete your personal data, in the app or by writing to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw, and we reply within the time the law sets. You can’t sign up without an email address; if you don’t agree to AI recognition you can still type contacts in.

People on cards

If a user has saved your details in BBCARD (for example you gave them your card), please contact that user first, or write to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw and we will help. If a data breach happens, we will tell those affected and report it to the authority as the law requires.

Export

You can export your roster from the app at any time (CSV or contact cards).

Keeping and deleting

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account. Settings › Privacy & terms › Delete data & account clears this phone and deletes your account, with your personal space’s roster and photos; what you added to a team stays with the team.
  • When you delete a contact it is removed from your roster and marked as deleted (so your other phones follow); the deleted contact and its card photo stay on our servers until you delete your account (personal space) or the team is deleted (team space), or until you ask us to erase them by writing to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw. The card readings the server keeps after recognition, and the activity log (who changed what, when), are kept the same way. The servers’ daily backups keep data up to 7 days longer.
  • Photos of org charts and photos that could not be read and were not saved as a contact stay in your space’s folder until you delete your account (in a team space: until the team is deleted).
  • A team owner has to hand the team over before deleting their account.
  • You can also write to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw to ask for your account and data to be deleted.
  • A team plan’s contract and billing records (company name, tax ID, invoice email, amounts) are kept as the law requires and are not deleted with an account.
  • A team’s data is kept for 90 days after its plan ends (to view and export); after that nobody can open it, and you can write to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw to have it deleted.
  • A member marked as left or removed from a team loses access to its roster from their next connection.

Security

Encrypted in transit (HTTPS); access rules on every record; an emailed code confirms who signs up; you can lock the app with your fingerprint or face.

Age

This is a business app for people 18 or older, and is not meant for children.
