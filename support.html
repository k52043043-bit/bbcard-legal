常見問題

收不到驗證碼

先看垃圾郵件匣，等一分鐘再按「重寄驗證碼」；還是收不到，請寄信給我們。

忘記密碼

在登入頁按「忘記密碼／還沒設過密碼」，輸入註冊的 Email，我們會寄一組驗證碼，再設一組新密碼。

名片辨識錯了

AI 辨識可能有錯。名片已經建好、但某個欄位讀錯了：打開那張名片，點欄位直接修改。整張讀不出來（照片太暗、反光或沒有整張入鏡）：重拍一次再掃。

我可以不同意 AI 辨識嗎？

可以。不同意也能使用：到「掃描」›「其他方式加人」自己輸入聯絡人；同意後可以隨時在「設定 › AI 辨識」撤回。

怎麼匯出名冊

在「聯絡人」分頁長按一位聯絡人進入選取，再點選其他人，然後點「匯出」，可以匯出成聯絡人名片檔（.vcf），從分享選單存進手機通訊錄，或匯出成 CSV。

Common questions

No sign-up code arrived

Check your spam folder, wait a minute and tap “Send the code again”; if it still doesn’t come, email us.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password / no password yet”, enter the email you signed up with, and we send you a code and let you set a new password.

A card was read wrongly

AI reading can be wrong. If a contact was created but a detail is wrong, open it and tap the field to correct it. If a photo couldn’t be read at all (too dark, glary or not showing the whole card), shoot it again and scan it.

Can I say no to AI reading?

Yes. You can still use the app and add contacts yourself under Scan › Other ways to add someone. If you agree, you can withdraw any time under Settings › AI recognition.

How to export your roster

On the Contacts tab long-press a contact to start selecting, tap the others, then tap “Export”: you get a contact file (.vcf) that you can add to your phone contacts from the share sheet, or a CSV.
