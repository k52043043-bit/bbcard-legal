BBCARD拍板 支援

有任何問題、回報錯誤或隱私相關的請求，請寫信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw，我們會盡快回覆。寫信時請附上註冊用的 Email，以及你在哪個畫面遇到什麼（有截圖更好）。

常見問題

  • 收不到驗證碼：先看垃圾郵件匣，等一分鐘再按「重寄驗證碼」；還是收不到，請寄信給我們。
  • 名片辨識錯了：AI 辨識可能有錯，打開那張名片，點欄位直接修改。照片太暗、反光或沒有整張入鏡會影響結果，重拍一次通常就好。
  • 怎麼匯出名冊：在「聯絡人」分頁長按一位聯絡人進入選取，再點選其他人，然後點「匯出」，可以存到手機通訊錄，或匯出成 CSV。
  • 怎麼刪除帳號與資料：見刪除帳號說明。
  • 我的資料被別人存進了 BBCARD：請先聯絡那位使用者；也可以寄信給我們，我們會協助。

BBCARD Support

For any question, bug report or privacy request, write to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw and we will reply as soon as we can. Please include the email you signed up with, and what you saw on which screen (a screenshot helps).

Common questions

  • No sign-up code arrived: check your spam folder, wait a minute and tap “Send the code again”; if it still doesn’t come, email us.
  • A card was read wrongly: AI reading can be wrong. Open the card and tap a field to correct it. A dark or glary photo, or one that doesn’t show the whole card, can hurt the result; shooting it again usually fixes it.
  • How to export your roster: on the Contacts tab long-press a contact to start selecting, tap the others, then tap “Export”, and save them to your phone contacts or export a CSV.
  • How to delete your account and data: see how to delete your account.
  • Your details were saved in BBCARD by someone else: please contact that user first, or email us and we will help.
