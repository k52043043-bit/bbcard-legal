刪除帳號與資料

在 App 裡刪除（最快）

  1. 打開 BBCARD拍板，點下方「設定」。
  2. 往下找到「隱私與條款」，點紅字的「刪除資料與帳號」。
  3. 在「帳號」那張卡片按「刪除帳號與所有資料」，再按「永久刪除」，帳號就會刪除。

會刪掉什麼、留下什麼

  • 會刪除：你的帳號、你個人空間的名冊和名片照片、你送出的意見回饋與截圖。
  • 會留下：你在團隊裡建立的資料屬於團隊，會留在團隊裡（不再顯示你的名字）。你是團隊負責人時，要先把團隊移交給別人才能刪除帳號；團隊裡只有你一個人、沒有人可移交時，請寫信給我們。
  • 伺服器的每日備份最多再保留 7 天。
  • 團隊方案的合約與帳單紀錄依法保存，不會隨帳號刪除。
  • 登入系統的登入事件紀錄（可能含 Email 與 IP 位址）由供應商保存；要清除請寫信給我們。

要多久

在 App 裡按下確認後，帳號會立刻刪除。個人空間的資料與照片在 30 天內從伺服器清除。

登不進去，或是想請我們代為處理

請用註冊的 Email 寄信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw，主旨寫「刪除帳號」，我們會在法定期限內處理，並回信告知。

Delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BBCARD and tap “Settings” at the bottom.
  2. Scroll to “Privacy & terms” and tap the red “Delete data & account”.
  3. On the “Account” card tap “Delete account and all data”, then “Delete permanently”, and the account is deleted.

What is deleted and what stays

  • Deleted: your account, your personal space’s roster and card photos, and the feedback and screenshots you sent.
  • Stays: what you added to a team belongs to the team and stays there (without your name). If you own a team, hand it over to someone else before deleting your account; if you are the only one on it and there is nobody to hand it to, write to us.
  • The servers’ daily backups keep it up to 7 days longer.
  • A team plan’s contract and billing records are kept as the law requires and are not deleted with an account.
  • Sign-in event records (possibly with your email and IP address) are kept by our sign-in provider; write to us to have them cleared.

How long it takes

The account is deleted as soon as you confirm in the app. The personal space’s data and photos are erased from our servers within 30 days.

Can’t sign in, or want us to do it for you

Write to k52043043@yahoo.com.tw from the email you signed up with, with the subject “Delete account”, and we will handle it within the time the law sets and reply to you.
