測試者 Android 教學

安裝 BBCARD拍板測試版（APK 安裝檔），約 10 分鐘。目前 Android 測試版：1.0.0（版本 19）。

流程
下載安裝檔、允許安裝、安裝、註冊登入、測 6 件事、回報問題
安裝檔
由賴泯佑直接傳給你（檔名像「BBCARD拍板_測試版_v1.0.0_code19_….apk」），不在 Google Play 上。
有問題找
賴泯佑。App 裡「設定」點「意見回饋」最快；也可以寄信到 k52043043@yahoo.com.tw

壹、先確認三件事

1-1 你的手機

  • Android 7.0 以上，64 位元（arm64）的手機；近幾年的手機幾乎都是。
  • 拍名片用 Google 的文件掃描器，需要 Google Play 服務；沒有的話會改用 App 自己的相機。

1-2 你需要準備

  • 賴泯佑傳給你的 .apk 安裝檔。
  • 一個收得到信的 Email（註冊 BBCARD 要收驗證碼）。
  • 網路（Wi-Fi 或行動網路都可以）。

1-3 先知道

這是「測試版」，還在開發，可能有錯誤或畫面不完整，這正是需要你幫忙找出來的地方。測試期間你輸入的資料會存在 BBCARD 的雲端。名片辨識時，照片會經 BBCARD 的伺服器交給 Google 的 AI（Gemini，必要時改由 Anthropic 的 Claude）讀出文字，所以請用你同意拍的名片，或用範例名片，不要拿客戶機密資料來試。細節見隱私權政策。

貳、安裝：4 步驟，約 5 分鐘

  1. 下載安裝檔：點賴泯佑傳給你的 .apk 檔，選「下載」。下載完成後，點通知，或到手機的「檔案」App 的「下載」找到它。
  2. 允許安裝：第一次會跳出「基於安全考量，不允許從這個來源安裝不明應用程式」之類的提示。點「設定」，打開「允許來自這個來源」（可能寫的是 LINE、Chrome 或檔案），再按返回鍵回到安裝畫面。
  3. 安裝：點「安裝」。如果出現 Google Play 安全防護的提示（找不到這個 App 的來源、要不要掃描），選「仍要安裝」（或先點「更多詳細資料」再選「仍要安裝」）。因為測試版不在 Google Play 上，才會有這個提示。
  4. 開啟：裝好後點「開啟」。之後桌面上會出現 BBCARD拍板 的圖示，和一般 App 一樣使用。

不同品牌的手機（Samsung、Pixel、小米、OPPO…）畫面上的字略有不同，意思一樣：允許安裝，再選「仍要安裝」。

之後有新版

賴泯佑會再傳新的安裝檔給你。直接點開安裝，會蓋過舊版，資料不會不見（不用先刪除舊版）。

參、第一次使用 BBCARD

3-1 註冊帳號

  1. 開啟後點「註冊新帳號」，輸入 Email，設定密碼（至少 8 個字，要有英文字母和數字）。
  2. 按「註冊，寄驗證碼」，到信箱找 6 位數驗證碼輸入，再按「完成註冊」。沒收到的話先看垃圾郵件。
  3. 進去之後選「個人使用」就能開始。如果賴泯佑給了 8 碼邀請碼，要加入團隊，就選「團隊使用」並輸入邀請碼。

3-2 第一次辨識前會先問你

  • 第一次拍名片或從相簿選照片時，App 會說明「名片照片會交給 Google 的 AI 辨識」：按「同意並繼續」。
  • 不同意也能用：到「掃描」›「其他方式加人」自己輸入聯絡人。同意後隨時可以在「設定 › AI 辨識」撤回。
  • 第一次用 Google 的掃描器要先下載一下，這段時間會先開 App 自己的相機（相機權限請按「允許」）。通知：每天提醒要用到，建議允許。

肆、請幫忙測這 6 件事

  1. 拍名片

    怎麼做點下方「掃描」，再點「拍名片」。Google 的掃描器會開啟：名片放桌上、手機對準，選「自動擷取」，框穩了就會自己拍。一疊名片就按「＋」再拍下一張，全部拍完按「繼續」。

    正常的樣子名片在背景上傳、辨識，1～2 分鐘後出現在「聯絡人」，姓名、公司、電話都對。

  2. 看名片卡

    怎麼做在「聯絡人」點一位。

    正常的樣子資料完整，打電話、寄信、地圖的按鈕點得動。

  3. 搜尋

    怎麼做在「聯絡人」上方搜尋框打字。也可以點搜尋框裡的麥克風，手機會跳出語音輸入，直接說話。

    正常的樣子找得到人。說「某某公司的決策者」會自動設好條件。

  4. 待辦與提醒

    怎麼做名片缺資料時，點「聯絡人」右下角的待辦，一位一位補。再到 BBCARD 的「設定」打開「每天提醒」（Android 13 以上會問要不要允許通知，請按允許）。

    正常的樣子隔天早上收到通知，點通知會打開「今天要聯絡」的清單。

  5. 關係圖

    怎麼做點下方「關係圖」，選一個客戶。

    正常的樣子看得到每家公司誰決策、誰影響，點人可以看資料。

  6. 語言與外觀

    怎麼做到「設定」，換語言、換淺色或深色。

    正常的樣子整個畫面馬上跟著換，沒有跑版或看不到的字。

圖為 Google 掃描器的畫面，名片資料是虛構的；你的手機上文字會跟著手機的語言。

不會用的功能，到「設定」點「使用教學」有圖文說明。免費版每月辨識 20 張，要多測可到「設定」的「方案」，用「測試版：免費試用」，不用付費。沒有名片可以用範例名片。

伍、日常使用

5-1 更新版本

有新版時賴泯佑會傳新的安裝檔，點開安裝即可（見上面「之後有新版」）。App 的「設定」最下面會寫目前的版本，可以對照。

5-2 遇到問題怎麼回報

  • 方法 A（最簡單）：直接在 BBCARD 裡回報。到「設定」點「意見回饋」，寫一兩句，可以附截圖（最多 3 張），按「送出」，會自動附上 App 版本和手機型號。
  • 方法 B：直接傳訊息給賴泯佑，附上截圖，並說明你的手機型號與 Android 版本。

回報時請寫三件事：你做了什麼（按了哪裡）、你以為會怎麼樣、實際發生了什麼（有截圖更好）。

5-3 不想再測了

  • 想刪掉 BBCARD 的帳號，到「設定」的「隱私與條款」，點紅字「刪除資料與帳號」。
  • 然後長按 BBCARD拍板 的圖示，選「解除安裝」。

陸、常見問題

出現「應用程式未安裝」或「套件似乎無效」

通常是檔案沒有下載完整：重新下載一次再裝。如果以前裝過簽章不同的版本，先把舊的 BBCARD 解除安裝再裝（資料在雲端，重新登入就回來）。還是不行，請告訴賴泯佑你的手機型號。

Google Play 安全防護擋下安裝

點「更多詳細資料」，再選「仍要安裝」。測試版不在 Google Play 上，所以會有這個提示。找不到這個選項時，請告訴賴泯佑你的手機型號。

找不到下載的檔案

打開手機的「檔案」App，看「下載」資料夾。

拍名片時開的不是 Google 的掃描器

第一次用，Google 的掃描器還在下載，會先開 App 自己的相機，這是正常的。過一會兒再試就會用 Google 的掃描器。

登入失敗、忘記密碼

在登入頁點「忘記密碼／還沒設過密碼」，用 Email 收驗證碼重設。沒收到驗證碼先看垃圾郵件。

辨識張數用完了

免費版每月 20 張。到「設定」的「方案」，用「測試版：免費試用」可以免費試更高的方案。

謝謝你幫忙測試！你回報的每一個問題，都會讓正式版更穩。

Android tester guide

Install the BBCARD test version from an APK file in about 10 minutes. Current Android test version: 1.0.0 (version 19).

Steps
Download the file, allow the install, install, sign up, try 6 things, report problems
The install file
Vito sends it to you directly (a file named like “BBCARD拍板_測試版_v1.0.0_code19_….apk”); it is not on Google Play.
Questions
Vito. The fastest way is Settings › Feedback inside the app; you can also email k52043043@yahoo.com.tw

1. Before you start

1-1 Your phone

  • Android 7.0 or later on a 64-bit (arm64) phone, which almost every phone of the last few years is.
  • Scanning uses Google’s document scanner, which needs Google Play services; without it the app’s own camera opens instead.

1-2 What you need

  • The .apk file Vito sent you.
  • An email address you can read (BBCARD sends a sign-up code).
  • An internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

1-3 Good to know

This is a test version still in development: it may have errors or unfinished screens, and finding them is exactly where we need your help. What you enter while testing is stored in BBCARD’s cloud. To read a card, its photo goes through BBCARD’s server to Google’s AI (Gemini, or Anthropic’s Claude if needed) that reads the text, so please use cards you are allowed to photograph, or the sample cards, and nothing confidential from a customer. Details are in the privacy policy.

2. Install: 4 steps, about 5 minutes

  1. Download the file: tap the .apk file Vito sent you and choose “Download”. When it is done, tap the notification, or find it in your phone’s Files app under “Downloads”.
  2. Allow the install: the first time the phone says something like “for your security, installing unknown apps from this source is not allowed”. Tap “Settings”, switch on “Allow from this source” (it may name LINE, Chrome or Files) and press Back to return to the install screen.
  3. Install: tap “Install”. If Google Play Protect shows a warning (unknown source, or an offer to scan), choose “Install anyway” (or tap “More details” first, then “Install anyway”). The warning appears because the test version is not on Google Play.
  4. Open: when it is done tap “Open”. A BBCARD icon appears on your home screen and works like any other app.

Phones from different makers (Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, OPPO…) word these screens a little differently, but the idea is the same: allow the install, then choose “Install anyway”.

When there is a new version

Vito will send you the new file. Just open it and install: it replaces the old version and your data stays (no need to delete the old one first).

3. Using BBCARD for the first time

3-1 Sign up

  1. Open the app and tap “Create an account”. Enter your email and choose a password (at least 8 characters, with letters and digits).
  2. Tap “Sign up and send the code”, find the 6-digit code in your inbox, enter it and tap “Finish signing up”. If it doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder.
  3. Choose “Personal use” and you are ready. If Vito gave you an 8-character invitation code to join a team, choose “Team use” and enter it.

3-2 It asks before the first reading

  • The first time you scan a card or pick a photo, the app explains that card photos are read by Google’s AI: tap “Agree and continue”.
  • You can say no and still use the app: add contacts yourself under Scan › Other ways to add someone. If you agree, you can withdraw at any time under Settings › AI recognition.
  • The first time, Google’s scanner has to download; until then the app’s own camera opens (tap “Allow” for the camera permission). Notifications: the daily reminder needs them, so please allow.

4. Please try these 6 things

  1. Scan a card

    HowTap “Scan” at the bottom, then “Scan cards”. Google’s document scanner opens: lay the card on a desk, point the phone at it and choose “Auto capture”; it shoots once the outline is steady. For a stack, tap “+” for the next card, and tap “Continue” when you are done.

    You should seeThe cards upload and are read in the background; after a minute or two they appear under “Contacts” with the right name, company and phone number.

  2. Open a contact

    HowOn the Contacts tab, tap a person.

    You should seeThe details are complete, and the call, email and map buttons work.

  3. Search

    HowType in the search box at the top of Contacts. You can also tap the microphone in the search box: the phone’s voice input opens, so just speak.

    You should seeYou find the person. Saying “the decision maker at (a company)” sets the filter for you.

  4. To-dos and reminders

    HowWhen a card is missing details, tap the to-do button at the bottom right of Contacts and fill them in one by one. Then turn on the daily reminder in BBCARD’s Settings (on Android 13 and later the phone asks whether to allow notifications: please allow).

    You should seeThe next morning you get a notification; tapping it opens the list of people to contact today.

  5. Relationship map

    HowTap “Map” at the bottom and pick a customer.

    You should seeYou see who decides and who influences at each company; tap a person for their details.

  6. Language and look

    HowIn Settings, switch the language and the light or dark look.

    You should seeThe whole screen follows at once, with nothing cut off or unreadable.

The pictures show Google’s scanner with a made-up card; its words follow your phone’s language.

For anything you are unsure of, open Settings › How to use for pictures and explanations. The free plan reads 20 cards a month; to test more, go to Settings › Plan and use “Test build: free trial”, no payment needed. No cards at hand? Use the sample cards.

5. Using it day to day

5-1 Updating

When there is a new version Vito sends a new file; open it to install (see “When there is a new version” above). The current version is shown at the bottom of Settings, so you can compare.

5-2 How to report a problem

  • Method A (easiest): report inside BBCARD. Go to Settings › Feedback, write a line or two, attach screenshots if you like (up to 3) and tap “Send”; the app version and phone model are added automatically.
  • Method B: message Vito directly with a screenshot, and say your phone model and Android version.

Please say three things: what you did (what you tapped), what you expected, and what actually happened (a screenshot helps).

5-3 When you want to stop

  • To delete your BBCARD account, go to Settings › Privacy & terms and tap the red “Delete data & account”.
  • Then touch and hold the BBCARD icon and choose “Uninstall”.

6. Common questions

It says “App not installed” or “The package appears to be invalid”

Usually the file did not download completely: download it again and retry. If you installed a version signed differently before, uninstall the old BBCARD first (your data is in the cloud and comes back when you sign in). If it still fails, tell Vito your phone model.

Google Play Protect blocks the install

Tap “More details”, then “Install anyway”. The warning appears because the test version is not on Google Play. If you can’t find that option, tell Vito your phone model.

I can’t find the downloaded file

Open your phone’s Files app and look in the “Downloads” folder.

The scanner that opens is not Google’s

The first time, Google’s scanner is still downloading, so the app’s own camera opens: that is normal. Try again a little later.

I can’t sign in or forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password / no password yet” and use the emailed code to set a new one. If no code arrives, check your spam folder.

The reading allowance ran out

The free plan reads 20 cards a month. Under Settings › Plan, “Test build: free trial” lets you try a higher plan for free.

Thank you for testing! Every problem you report makes the real version more solid.
